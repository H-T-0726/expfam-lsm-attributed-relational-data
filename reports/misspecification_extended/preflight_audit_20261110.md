## Model and evaluation audit

- FACT: M1 generates `n=75, d=12, K=3`, X families `G3/B6/P3`, Bernoulli Y, Gaussian X variance 1, `f_scale=sqrt(2)`, `w0=-1`, `w=1`, with data seeds 1001001–1001020 (`run_misspecification_m1.py`). The generator draws a common Z and F, then X column by column, then Y (`data_generator_canonical_mixed.py`).
- FACT: `em_runner.run_em_experimental` dispatches `family_x_list` and `numerics_mode="consistent"` to `DualExpFamLSMPerColumnConsistent`. This is experimental prototype lineage E and is not manuscript-adopted. Its E-step samples Z; the M-step updates F, Gaussian X dispersion, w0 and w (`em_runner.py` around lines 249–300). `failure_policy="fail_fast"` prohibits retries and replacement. `validate_support=True` and `allow_support_mismatch=False` reject incompatible support (`diagnostics.py`). The same model seed controls initialization and MC draws unless the optional split seeds are provided; shared seeds do not prove identical random streams across families.
- FACT: Bernoulli→Poisson uses 0/1 observations within Poisson support. Bernoulli→Gaussian and Poisson→Gaussian use finite observations within Gaussian support; they are discrete-to-continuous modeling approximations. The observed X is never transformed. The compared endpoint is Z error, not raw likelihood across different measures. Poisson→Bernoulli was not requested and would generally fail support.
- FACT: M1 uses the final MC sample and rotation-only Procrustes RMSE (`utils_expfam.py`), with dense=2497 and sparse=250 training pairs after a common 278-pair test split. E1 uses the same fit path, scores final sample, final-five mean, and a fixed-theta joint posterior mode from the final sample (`laplace_k_criterion.py`, gradient tolerance 1e-8, max 200 iterations). It records gradient and minimum Hessian eigenvalue. E1 Part A reproduced 480 M1 values exactly according to `full/reproduction.json`.
- OBSERVED (E1, **same lineage, historical results only**): 160/160 fits and 320/320 mode solves succeeded. Mode ΔRMSE was dense +0.015/+0.009 and sparse +0.076/+0.087 for seed series A/B (20 replicates each). Last-sample dense effect changed from +0.020 to +0.002 across seeds. This motivates mode as the prespecified primary endpoint here; mode is not assumed generally optimal.

## Frozen design decisions

The machine-readable `protocol.json` and full `manifest.csv` are generated before the first new fit. Five principal arms (correct, B6→P6, B6→G6, P3→G3, all G) cross four nested Y rates, 20 data draws and two E1 seed series: 800 fit cells. Six intermediate column-count arms cross 100% and 10% Y: 480 more cells. Of 1,280 unique cells, 160 correct/B6→P6 cells at 100%/10% can be reused from E1 only after provenance and key checks; 1,120 new fits remain. Baseline cells are shared, not counted repeatedly. New column order uses a dedicated deterministic seed per replicate and source family, with nested prefixes. M1's exact sparse mask is retained as the 10% set; new 25% and 50% sets extend it with a separate deterministic permutation of the remaining pool.

Primary endpoints are absolute rotation-only mode RMSE and paired Δ=wrong−correct. Secondary endpoints are final-sample and mean-five RMSE, Gram correlation, norm ratio, held-out Bernoulli log score/AUC/AP, w/w0 errors, warnings, runtime, and mode gradient/curvature. Failed fits and non-OK modes remain in the manifest and results; pairs require two valid mode results. There is no sign-based success threshold. Technical validity means the preflight contracts, finite metrics, and positive curvature/stationarity of the mode pass. Scientific outcome can favor either arm. Reproduction tolerance for replayed M1/E1 metrics is 1e-12 exact and 1e-6 for an investigated floating-point difference; no source CSV is changed. Inference uses data replicate as the independent unit, with both model seeds nested inside it.

## Limits known before execution

HYPOTHESIS: the cost of wrong X families may increase as Y pairs decrease or as more columns are wrong. Neither monotonicity nor the ranking of wrong families is assumed. Column subset choice may affect the estimates. The mode is conditional on estimated parameters and can find a local solution. Prototype results require a separate human decision before manuscript adoption. If pilot timing makes the full 1,120 new fits infeasible in this session, the manifest and checkpointed results will show the exact unfinished cells; no partial result will be called complete.
